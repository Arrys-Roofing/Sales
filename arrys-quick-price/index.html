What changed from the spreadsheet

  • Overhead on every direct costPermits, dump and warranty now carry overhead. The workbook pointed at an empty cell, so those costs were left out.
  • One overhead rateEvery roof type reads the same factor. Three sheets in the workbook pulled it from separate files.
  • Waste on the covering onlyWaste applies to the roof material, not to boots and nails.
  • Margin you keepShows what's left after card fees and commission, next to the target margin.
