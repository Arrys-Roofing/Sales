0 2 · The Shingle·LayerLock · StrikeZone

The same nailing zone, more shingle.

LayerLock is what makes it install right.

GAF mechanically fastens the two laminate layers together during manufacturing. That is LayerLock, and it creates the StrikeZone nailing area, which GAF calls the industry’s widest at about six hundred percent larger than the old Timberline HD.

It matters because a shingle blows off when a nail misses. In Home Innovation Research Labs testing on a sixteen square deck a roofer placed 999 of 1,000 nails correctly, and GAF puts the fastening speed at up to thirty percent faster.

0 6 · The Details·Wind · warranty · colour

What it is actually warranted to.

The numbers, not the adjectives.

Wind ratings on a shingle are warranty figures, not a limit on the material. GAF covers UHDZ to 130 mph installed with four nails and a GAF starter strip at the eaves and rakes, and to 110 mph without that starter strip.

The strip costs very little and doubles as the difference between those two numbers, which is a good illustration of how the whole GAF system works.

  • What is WindProven?
    A fifteen year GAF wind warranty with no maximum wind speed and, GAF says, no named-storm exclusion. It needs a LayerLock shingle plus starter strip, roof deck protection, ridge cap, and either a leak barrier or attic ventilation.
  • Is it worth it over HDZ?
    If you want the heavier shingle, the deeper shadow line, Class 4 impact and the longer algae and workmanship terms, yes. If you want the most roof for the money, HDZ is still a very good roof.
  • Does it come in more colours?
    Six, which is fewer than HDZ. If the colour you want is not among them, that is a real reason to stay with HDZ rather than compromise on the look.
  • What are the black streaks on Florida roofs?
    Algae, not dirt. Both shingles carry time-release copper granules to fight it. UHDZ carries ten percent more of them and five more years of warranty against it.
  • Who can install it?
    Anyone, but the top warranties are not open to everyone. Silver Pledge needs a GAF Certified Plus or Master Elite contractor, and Golden Pledge needs Master Elite, which GAF puts at about two percent of roofers in North America.
  • Is it heavier on my structure?
    Twenty percent more than HDZ, which is not a structural question on a normal framed roof. If your roof has already carried two layers of shingle it is worth a look before anyone specifies anything.
