0 4 · Your Questions·For the board

The questions a board asks.

Answered before they are asked, including the ones with an uncomfortable answer.

  • Can we keep meeting while this is going on?
    Yes. This is the reason for the system. There is no tear-off of the existing roof, so there is no day when the building is open to the sky, and GAF describes the method as low odour and low noise. We agree the sequence with your office before anything starts, and we work around Sunday mornings and the chapel’s weekend bookings.
  • What happens in a hurricane?
    Read this one carefully, because it is the answer most proposals leave out. The guarantee covers leaks from winds up to 55 mph as standard. GAF does offer enhanced coverage from 60 to 120 mph, but it is not automatic — it requires GAF’s advance written approval and it is priced separately. If the board wants it, say so and we will take it to GAF before the contract is signed.
  • We have equipment on the roof. Do the technicians void anything?
    Not any more. Rooftop traffic without GAF walkways is a stated exclusion on the guarantee, which is why GAF walkway pads at the rooftop equipment are included in this price rather than sold to you later. Your HVAC contractor can walk the roof for twenty years without touching the coverage.
  • Who do we call in year twelve?
    Us first, at the same Tarpon Springs office. The guarantee is backed by the contractor and the manufacturer together, and because workmanship is inside it there is no argument to be had about whether a leak is the material’s fault or the installer’s. That argument is the one a materials-only warranty leaves you to have.
  • What is not in the price?
    Wood. Rotted or damaged decking and framing is the one item priced outside the number, at the rates on the Unforeseen Conditions and Extras Form, and only where it is found. Permits, dump fees, workers’ compensation, general liability and taxes are all inside the number.
  • Does the guarantee survive if the church ever sells the property?
    It transfers. GAF requires the written request within 60 days of the sale, any repairs its inspection identifies to be completed, and a $500 assignment fee.

0 5 · The Product·GAF EverGuard® 60 mil fleece-back

A membrane with a back to it.

What fleece-back is, and why it is the sheet for a building that stays open.

TPO is a white single-ply membrane, hot-air welded at the seams. Fleece-back is the same membrane with a polyester fleece laminated to its underside, and that layer is the whole reason this system was specified for your roof.

The fleece does three jobs at once. It separates the new membrane from what is under it, so nothing bleeds through and stains. It removes the need for a cover board or slip sheet. And it takes an impact the bare sheet would not.

GAF publishes the 60 mil fleece-back at 426 lb puncture resistance, and this roof takes 430 squares of it. That figure is about your rooftop equipment: churches carry HVAC, HVAC carries technicians, and technicians carry tools they drop.

0 6 · The Scope·TPO Flat Roof System: Fully Adhered

Exactly what we install.

Nothing here is an allowance or a to-be-determined.

This is the full scope of work, in the contract’s own words, with what each line is for underneath it.

  1. 0 1

    Leave existing single layer roof system down to substrate on flat roof.

    The existing single layer stays where it is and becomes the base for the new one, with any failed areas cut out and made good first. That is what a fleece-back membrane is built to be adhered over, and it is what keeps the tear-off, the noise and the open roof out of your week.

  2. 0 2

    Replace any rotted or damaged wood as needed at an additional cost (Please review Unforeseen Conditions/Extras Form for woodwork prices)

    Nobody can price wood they cannot see until the roof is opened. The rates are agreed in writing on the Extras Form before any of it is done, so there is no discovered number.

  3. 0 3

    Inspect existing deck fasteners and re-nail wood decking per current Florida and Local Building Codes as needed using 2 3/8″ 8D ring shank nails.

    A membrane is only as fixed down as the deck under it. Ring shank nails hold where smooth nails back out, and re-nailing to current code is what makes the deck a legal substrate rather than an old one.

  4. 0 4

    Install fully adhered attached GAF 60 mil Fleece-back TPO system.

    Fully adhered means the sheet is bonded across its whole area rather than held down at points, so there is no field of fasteners through the membrane and nothing for wind to lift and flutter.

  5. 0 5

    Install GAF TPO boots and vents for existing

    Every pipe and vent through a flat roof is a hole in it. TPO boots weld into the membrane as one continuous surface, which is a different thing entirely from a collar sealed with mastic.

  6. 0 6

    Install TPO up and over all parapet walls and cover with coping cap.

    A parapet takes weather on both faces and is where wind-driven rain gets in. Running the membrane up and over the top, then capping it, means the wall is inside the waterproofing rather than beside it.
