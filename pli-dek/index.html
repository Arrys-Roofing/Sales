A balcony is a roof.

The Pli-Dek® Deck System · A Tampa Bay homeowner’s guide
Manufacturer-trained applicator · ICC-ES ESR-2097

If there is a room under your balcony, that balcony is a roof. Water through it lands on framing, insulation and drywall, and nobody sees it until the ceiling stains.

Paint and traffic coatings sit on top of the wood. Pli-Dek builds a reinforced cement walking surface that waterproofs the structure and is the finished floor at the same time.

It is code listed under ICC-ES ESR-2097, carries Florida Product Approval FL15027, and can only be installed by an applicator the manufacturer has trained.

0 2 · The System·ESR-2097 · FL15027

Not a coating. An assembly.

Ten layers, a fraction of an inch thick.

Over the plywood go seam paper at every joint, flashings at every wall, door, post and penetration, then G60 hot-dip galvanized lath. A polymer-modified cement base coat is poured over that lath and encapsulates it.

The result is one continuous reinforced surface rather than a stack of fastened panels. On top go a waterproof membrane, an intermediate coat with a fibreglass mat, a texture coat, colour, and an optional clear sealer.

The Pli-Dek and Con-Dek systems must be installed by applicators trained and approved by Pli-Dek LLC.ICC-ES ESR-2097

Where it goes, and why it lasts.

Elevated living space, over living space.

Pli-Dek is for pedestrian surfaces built over something you care about: balconies, breezeways, exterior stairs, condo walkways and rooftop decks. Anywhere people walk on wood framing with a room underneath.

In Florida the enemy is not one storm. It is rain, salt air and humidity working on the same joint for years, and corrosion on the metal supporting it. Both stay hidden until they are expensive.

  • How thick is it?
    A fraction of an inch, and it adds very little dead load. That is why it suits wood-framed balconies and renovation work where a tile assembly would be too heavy.
  • Can anybody install it?
    No, and this is not a preference. The ICC evaluation report itself requires applicators trained and approved by the manufacturer, so an untrained install is not a code-compliant one.
  • What about fire?
    One hour fire rated with a Class A fire rating. The Pli-Dek F system additionally carries California State Fire Marshal listing 8110-2327:0500 for wildland urban interface areas.
  • Is it approved in Florida?
    Yes. Florida Product Approval FL15027, and ICC-ES ESR-2097 was reissued in August 2026 with compliance running through August 2027.
  • How is it different from a deck coating?
    A coating is a film on top of wood. This is a reinforced cement assembly with the lath embedded in it, so the deck works as one continuous surface rather than a floor plus a separate waterproofing layer.
  • Does it come in colours?
    Colour coats, stains and sealers, with texture from smooth to heavy knock-down. Confirm against physical samples and the manufacturer’s chart rather than a screen.
